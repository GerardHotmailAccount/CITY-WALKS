"Open my heart and you will see, graved inside of it, 'Italy.' Rome is the city of visible history, where the past and present are tied together in a beautiful, chaotic knot. Every stone has a story, and every alleyway leads to a fountain that has bubbled for centuries."

- Robert Browning, poet

"Venice is like eating an entire box of chocolate liqueurs in one go. It is an enchanted, watery maze where the streets are made of emerald glass. To wander here is to lose track of time, drifting between grand palaces and secret bridges where the only sound is the water."

- Truman Capote, writer

"Paris is always a good idea. It is a city that doesn't just offer sights, but a feeling of belonging to something timeless. From the scent of fresh baguettes on a rainy morning to the golden glow of Montmartre at sunset, it whispers that life is meant to be savored slowly."

- Audrey Hepburn, actress
Seville

"Seville is a pleasant city, famous for its oranges and its people. But beyond the fruit, it is a place of fire and passion. The air smells of orange blossoms and jasmine, and the sound of a lone Spanish guitar echoing off ancient Moorish walls tells you that you’ve truly arrived."

- Lord Byron, poet
Porto

"Porto is best discovered through its flavors and its grit. It is a city of granite and gold, where the morning mist clings to the Douro and a local tavern serves the legendary Francesinha—a sandwich so rich and layered it tells a deeper story of Porto’s indulgence than any palace ever could."

- José Mourinho, Porto Guide
